Theory Audit Decision — Frame the Project as Spatial Recognition Systems Theory:
The project should remain grounded in GIS, spatial science, and place-based systems. The broad framework should be framed as Spatial Recognition Systems Theory, with Recognition Transformation describing the conversion of latent spatial recognizability capacity into realized recognition mass, and Recognition Disequilibrium describing failures or distortions in that transformation. Broader recognition literatures may inform the work, but the core theory should not drift into political, philosophical, perceptual, or non-spatial recognition theory.
