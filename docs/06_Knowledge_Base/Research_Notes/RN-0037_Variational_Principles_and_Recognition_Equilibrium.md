Layer 37 Decision — Recognition Equilibrium May Be Variational:
Recognition equilibrium may be defined as the set of recognition states minimizing a system-specific disequilibrium energy functional. This functional should combine local realization mismatch, allocation consistency, recognition geometry, transfer/shadowing structure, and system constraints only where each term corresponds to an explicit recognition principle. Variational equilibrium is promising because it derives equilibrium from the theory rather than assuming it externally, but it should remain provisional until the functional form is justified.
