Layer 7 Decision — Equilibrium as Stable Recognition Allocation:
Recognition equilibrium is provisionally defined as the set of stable recognition allocation states compatible with latent recognition potential and the constraints of a recognition system. Equilibrium does not require observed recognition to equal recognition potential everywhere. It requires that recognition be allocated in a stable, system-consistent way given opportunity, transmission, competition, history, uncertainty, and time horizon. Disequilibrium is therefore a persistent or meaningful deviation from this equilibrium set, not merely a statistical residual.
