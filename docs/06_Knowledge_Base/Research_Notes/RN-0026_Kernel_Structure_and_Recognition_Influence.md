Layer 26 Decision — Recognition Kernel as Signed, Directed, Mechanism-Bearing Influence:
The recognition kernel k
R
	​

(x,y) is provisionally defined as the signed, directed, system-conditioned influence of recognition associated with y on the recognition state of x. It may encode reinforcement, spillover, shadowing, substitution, competition, or suppression. The kernel should not be assumed symmetric, nonnegative, normalized, or purely distance-decaying unless those properties are justified by the recognition system.
