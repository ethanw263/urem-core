Layer 38 Decision — Recognition Functionals Must Encode Recognition Principles:
RDE may define system-level recognition functionals to measure disequilibrium, allocation-realization mismatch, transport misallocation, interaction energy, and concentration. These functionals should not become arbitrary weighted scores. Each term must correspond to an explicit recognition principle such as allocation consistency, recognition reallocation cost, mechanism-bearing interaction, or recognition concentration. The most promising initial functional combines allocation-realization mismatch with recognition-geometry-aware transport misallocation.
