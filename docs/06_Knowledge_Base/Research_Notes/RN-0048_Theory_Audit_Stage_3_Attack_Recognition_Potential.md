Theory Audit Decision — Recognition Potential Survives Only as System-Conditioned Recognizability Capacity:
Recognition Potential Φ is vulnerable to collapse into suitability, utility, attractiveness, expected recognition, or latent demand if defined too broadly. It survives the audit only when defined as system-conditioned recognizability capacity: the latent capacity of an object to become legible, valued, transmissible, and durable as a recognized object within a structured recognition system. Suitability, utility, attractiveness, demand, and expected recognition may contribute to or estimate aspects of Φ, but none is equivalent to it.
