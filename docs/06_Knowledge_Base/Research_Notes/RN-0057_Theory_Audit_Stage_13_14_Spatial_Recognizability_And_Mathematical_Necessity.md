Theory Audit Decision — Spatial Recognizability Requires Measure, Probability, and Relational Structure First:
Latent spatial recognizability is distinct from suitability because it concerns a place’s capacity to become legible, distinguishable, nameable, comparable, transmissible, memorable, and assignable within a spatial recognition system. The mathematics required by this object begins with measure theory, probability, and graph/relational structure. Optimization, optimal transport, and operator theory are promising extensions, while PDEs, topology, and differential geometry should remain speculative unless later justified.
