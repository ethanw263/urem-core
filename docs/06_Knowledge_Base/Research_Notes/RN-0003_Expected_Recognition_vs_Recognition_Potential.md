Step 4 — Immediate Research Questions

Before writing equations, we should answer:

Is Φ(x) purely local, or partly relational?
Can Φ(x) exist even when R(x)=0?
Can two places have equal physical potential but different recognition potential?
Can recognition potential decay, saturate, or transfer?
Is Φ(x) domain-specific or general?
Is expected recognition estimating Φ(x), or estimating realized recognition under current system constraints?
Is disequilibrium the gap between Φ(x) and R(x), or between expected R(x) and observed R(x)?

Layer 5 Decision — Recognition Potential as Latent System-Conditioned Capacity:
Recognition Potential Φ(x;R) is provisionally defined as the latent capacity of a location or spatial object to generate, retain, or transmit recognition within a specified recognition system. It is distinct from physical suitability, expected recognition, observed recognition, and probability of occurrence. Its purpose is to support a theory of unrealized recognition capacity rather than another suitability score or residual index.
