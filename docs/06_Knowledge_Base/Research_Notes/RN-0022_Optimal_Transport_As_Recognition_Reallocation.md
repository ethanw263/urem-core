Layer 22 Decision — Optimal Transport as Recognition Reallocation Geometry:
Optimal transport is a promising mathematical structure for RDE because it formalizes recognition misallocation as the cost of transforming observed recognition distribution into potential-consistent recognition distribution. Balanced OT may be useful for fixed-attention systems, while unbalanced or constrained OT may be more appropriate when recognition can expand, decay, or move through system-specific geographies, networks, or comparison spaces. OT should be treated as a candidate geometry of misallocation, not as the full theory itself.
