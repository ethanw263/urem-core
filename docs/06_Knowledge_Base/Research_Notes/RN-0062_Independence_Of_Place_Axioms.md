Axiomatic Decision — Spatial Grounding and Persistent Identifiability Are Independent:
Spatial grounding and persistent identifiability are independent admissibility conditions for spatial places. Spatial grounding alone does not create a place, because coordinates or raster cells may lack place identity. Persistent identity alone does not create a spatial place, because many identifiable entities are non-spatial. Therefore an admissible spatial place is provisionally defined as an entity that is both geographically grounded and persistently identifiable within a spatial recognition system.
